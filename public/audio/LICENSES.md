# Ambient field recordings

These files are bundled locally. Playback makes no requests to an outside service.
All four recordings are released under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), permitting redistribution and modification without attribution. Credits are retained here anyway. Source pages and licenses checked 2026-11-23.

| Bundled file | Original / author | Source page | Download |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| birds.mp3 | Bird chirping sounds — syncopika | https://opengameart.org/content/bird-chirping-sounds | https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/birdchirping071414.wav |
| crickets.mp3 | Crickets Ambient Noise - loopable — Wolfgang_ / Ted Kerr | https://opengameart.org/content/crickets-ambient-noise-loopable | https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/crickets_1.mp3 |

| rain.mp3 | Rain (loopable), sample 2 — Ylmir | https://opengameart.org/content/rain-loopable | https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Rain%20MP3.zip |

Processing: FFmpeg loudnorm at -23 LUFS / -3 dBTP, mono 32 kHz MP3 (80 kbps birds, 64 kbps others), short endpoint fades to soften nature loops; bird calls are spaced with silence in a 12-second loop rather than an incessant bed, and rain retains its authored loop with a brief entry fade. The initial insect-heavy bird ambience was replaced after runtime listening review with this clearly identifiable backyard bird recording. No paid sound API, music, tracking or third-party runtime dependency. Optional propeller and filtered winter wind are original WebAudio synthesis, not field recordings.

The wildlife models are original procedural geometry in this repository, not downloaded assets.
