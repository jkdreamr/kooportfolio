SKY MODEL

Positions are computed locally from the device clock's UTC timestamp and the
selected world's latitude/longitude, with proper motion, IAU 1976 precession,
Greenwich mean sidereal time and equatorial-to-horizontal conversion.
Reference: https://aa.usno.navy.mil/faq/GAST
Reference: https://aa.usno.navy.mil/faq/alt_az
Solar approximation: https://aa.usno.navy.mil/faq/sun_approx

Live updates every 30 seconds. Tonight is a clearly marked preview at 22:00
on today's date in that destination's timezone (including DST). Real solar
altitude gates live star visibility after civil twilight, independent of the
village's artistic lighting presets. Stars below the horizon never render.
Stanford and Home use the same Bay Area coordinates; Seoul uses its own.

This is a wide-field clear-sky star chart, not a literal optical telescope
simulation or observatory-grade prediction. It does not model local weather,
light pollution, atmospheric refraction/extinction, nutation, aberration,
parallax, variable-star brightness, planets, satellites or the Moon. Colors
and point sizes are display approximations of catalogue color/magnitude.
